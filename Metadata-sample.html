About This Video

Overview

World's first Pokémon-themed in-flight safety video. Launched August 15, 2024 on Pikachu Jet NH (Boeing 787-9) and Eevee Jet NH (Boeing 777-200ER). Expanded to all ANA domestic and international flights December 1, 2024.

Production

3D Pokémon characters overlaid on actual aircraft footage shot using a real aircraft with ANA cabin attendants and staff involved in the production. Produced in collaboration with The Pokémon Company as part of the Pokémon Air Adventures partnership launched March 2023.

Accessibility

Features cutaway bubble with Japanese Sign Language and International Sign instructions, adopted in cooperation with the Japanese Federation of the Deaf. Subtitles available in 18 languages including language of country of service for international flights.

Distribution

Expected use until end of August 2028. Star Wars livery aircraft excluded — continue showing original safety videos. YouTube release surpassed 1 million combined views.

Source

ANA Holdings press release (August 15, 2024). ANA Pokémon Air Adventures page. The Pokémon Company corporate topics.

About This Video

Overview

Innovative flight safety film inspired by Delftware decoration techniques. More than 1,000 handmade Delft Blue tiles were specially drawn, animated, and painted with utmost precision for this production.

Production

The script was translated into handmade Delftware drawings, painted onto tiles, then filmed using stop-motion animation — tile by tile. Produced by Pedri Animation BV with agency DDB & Tribal. D.O.P.: Tom Driesen. Visual style: Dimitri Versteegen. Animator: Jurjen Kraan.

Design Context

Part of broader Dutch design initiative including the Economy Class "Tasty Blue" catering concept and KLM's tradition of Delftware miniature houses dating to the 1950s. Visual-first animation design minimizes language dependency for international audiences.

Distribution

Premiered at Dutch Design Week Eindhoven, October 17–25, 2015. Screened on all KLM intercontinental flights from November 1, 2015.

Source

KLM News (October 16, 2015). Pedri Animation BV production credits. KLM Facebook making-of post.
